Our story

Rooted in the neighborhood

Good bread brings people together.

North Star Bakery celebrates the comforting ritual of baking: starting early, mixing simple ingredients, and sharing fresh food with the community.

Our bakery's story is about the care behind each loaf and the familiar feeling of a neighborhood gathering place.
